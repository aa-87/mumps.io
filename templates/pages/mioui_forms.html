Dense SSR form rules

  • Shape labels, help, and errors on the server so pages stay predictable.
  • Prefer compact groups and sticky action bars for operator-heavy pages.
  • Use select fields for stable enumerations and toggles for binary workflow flags.
  • Keep form sections short and readable. Dense does not mean cluttered.
