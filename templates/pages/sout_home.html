THE APPROACH02 — 04

Begin with the familiar.
Go beyond it.

Arabic can feel like a world away when the letters and sounds are new. Our approach gives English speakers a first foothold with familiar pronunciation cues—then builds toward the real sounds, script and rhythm of Arabic.

Not a shortcut around the language. A more welcoming way into it.
